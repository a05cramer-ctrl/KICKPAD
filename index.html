    Rules

    Read the tongue.

    Everything that decides who gets a pair, printed where sneakers keep their size tag.

    KICKPADRULES · RN 0412
    Fees
    90% to the coin's box, 10% buys and burns $KICKS. The split is locked on pump.fun at launch.
    Target
    Lowest ask for the exact size, plus the marketplace fee and shipping. Read again the moment the box fills.
    Buy
    Deadstock only, from a verified marketplace. Receipt posted.
    Tickets
    One per 0.01% of supply held at the fill slot. Pools, the box wallet and the dev wallet don't count.
    Draw
    sha256(blockhash : mint : box) mod tickets
    Claim
    72 hours. Sign with the winning wallet. Ship the pair or take the box value in SOL.
    Unclaimed
    Redrawn from the next slot.
    After
    The box resets and fills again for the next pair.
